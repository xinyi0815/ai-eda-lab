Now recruiting M.S. and Ph.D. students (IPSI & Institute of Electronics) and undergraduate project students — see Join Us. 誠徵碩博士生（前瞻所、電子所）與專題生 — 詳見加入實驗室。

We work on electronic design automation and its collision with machine learning — analog layout automation, physical design, 3D-IC power delivery, and language models that read schematics and write design data. Recent lab results include the Best Artifact Award at MLCAD 2025 for Netlistify, papers at DAC and ISPD, two prizes at the ICCAD 2024 CAD Contest, and a U.S. patent issued in 2025. The lab is led by Prof. Mark Po-Hung Lin.

我們研究電子設計自動化（EDA）與機器學習的交會 — 類比佈局自動化、實體設計、3D-IC 供電網路，以及能讀懂電路圖、產生設計資料的語言模型。實驗室近期成果包括 Netlistify 獲 MLCAD 2025 最佳成果獎、DAC 與 ISPD 論文、ICCAD 2024 CAD 競賽兩獎，以及 2025 年獲證的美國專利。實驗室由林柏宏教授主持。

    AI and language models for design automation

    AI 與大型語言模型輔助設計自動化

    Language models are starting to read specifications, write RTL and reason about layout the way a designer does. We study where that actually holds up — applying LLMs to chip and PCB design tasks, and building the benchmarks needed to tell real capability from fluent guessing. Recent papers →

    語言模型已開始能閱讀規格、撰寫 RTL、像設計者一樣推理佈局。我們研究這件事在什麼情況下真的成立 — 將 LLM 應用於晶片與 PCB 設計任務，並建立能區分「真實能力」與「流暢猜測」的評測基準。相關論文 →

    Analog and mixed-signal layout automation

    類比與混合訊號佈局自動化

    Analog layout resists automation because so much of it is unwritten: symmetry, matching, proximity, parasitic sensitivity. We formalize those constraints and generate layouts that respect them — symmetry-island placement, common-centroid structures, parasitic-aware sizing — work behind several of the lab's 9 U.S. patents and its DAC and ICCAD papers.

    類比佈局難以自動化，是因為大量知識從未被寫下來：對稱、匹配、鄰近性、寄生效應敏感度。我們將這些限制形式化並自動產生符合的佈局 — 對稱島擺置、共質心結構、考慮寄生效應的尺寸設計 — 這些工作是實驗室 9 件美國專利與多篇 DAC、ICCAD 論文的基礎。

    Physical design automation

    實體設計自動化

    Placement, legalization and routing at modern densities — most recently multibit flip-flop banking for power–timing co-optimization (DATE 2026) and GPU-accelerated logic rewriting, which took 2nd place in the ICCAD 2024 CAD Contest.

    現代密度下的擺置、合法化與繞線 — 近期包括多位元正反器合併以共同最佳化功耗與時序（DATE 2026），以及在 ICCAD 2024 CAD 競賽獲第二名的 GPU 加速邏輯改寫。

    3D integration and advanced packaging

    三維積體電路與先進封裝

    Stacking dies changes what counts as a good floorplan. Our DAC 2024 work generates multiple power planes on redistribution layers of 3D ICs with Voronoi diagrams.

    晶片堆疊改變了「好的平面規劃」的定義。DAC 2024 的工作以 Voronoi 圖在 3D IC 的重佈線層上生成多重電源平面。

    Schematic understanding and document analysis

    電路圖理解與文件分析

    Decades of circuit knowledge sit locked in images — scanned schematics, datasheet figures, patent drawings. Netlistify, Best Artifact Award at MLCAD 2025, turns circuit schematics back into netlists with deep learning.

    數十年的電路知識被鎖在圖片裡 — 掃描電路圖、規格書插圖、專利圖說。獲 MLCAD 2025 最佳成果獎的 Netlistify 以深度學習將電路圖轉回網表。

    Joining the lab加入實驗室

    We are recruiting highly motivated Ph.D. and M.S. students (through the Institute of Pioneer Semiconductor Innovation and the Institute of Electronics), undergraduate project students, and full-time research assistants who want to work on hard electronic design automation problems at the intersection with AI. Competitive stipends are offered for qualified candidates.

    To apply, email Prof. Lin (mphlin@nycu.edu.tw) with your resume, transcripts and anything that shows what you can do.

    實驗室徵求對 EDA 與 AI 交會領域有熱忱的博士生、碩士生（前瞻所、電子所）、專題生與專任研究助理，並提供具競爭力的研究津貼。

    申請方式：將履歷、成績單與任何能展現你能力的資料，以電子郵件寄給林柏宏老師（mphlin@nycu.edu.tw）。
