What we work on研究領域

Electronic design automation is the software layer between a circuit idea and a manufacturable chip. It is also one of the last engineering disciplines where a great deal of expert judgment has never been written down — analog layout, floorplanning under competing constraints, reading a schematic and knowing what it does.

The lab works on both halves of that problem: classical optimization for placement, routing and 3D integration, and learning-based methods, including large language models, for the parts that resisted formalization. Our results appear at DAC, ICCAD, DATE, ISPD and MLCAD, and several have become issued patents and contest-winning tools.

電子設計自動化（EDA）是介於電路構想與可製造晶片之間的軟體層，也是少數至今仍有大量專家判斷未被書面化的工程領域 —— 類比佈局、多重限制下的平面規劃、看懂一張電路圖並理解它在做什麼，都屬於這一類。

實驗室同時處理這個問題的兩面：用傳統最佳化方法解決擺置、繞線與三維整合，並用機器學習方法（包含大型語言模型）處理那些難以形式化的部分。研究成果發表於 DAC、ICCAD、DATE、ISPD、MLCAD 等會議，並已轉化為多項美國專利與競賽奪冠的工具。

AI and language models for design automation

AI 與大型語言模型輔助設計自動化

Language models and learning-based agents are starting to read specifications, write RTL and reason about layout the way a designer does. We study where that actually holds up — applying LLMs to chip and PCB design tasks, and building the benchmarks needed to tell real capability from fluent guessing.

語言模型與學習式代理人已開始能閱讀規格、撰寫 RTL，並像設計者一樣推理佈局。我們研究這件事在什麼情況下真的成立 —— 將 LLM 應用於晶片與 PCB 設計任務，並建立能區分「真實能力」與「流暢猜測」的評測基準。

Analog and mixed-signal layout automation

類比與混合訊號佈局自動化

Analog layout resists automation because so much of it is unwritten: symmetry, matching, proximity, parasitic sensitivity. We formalize those constraints and generate layouts that respect them — placement on symmetry islands, common-centroid structures, parasitic-aware capacitor sizing — work that has produced both top-tier papers and issued U.S. patents.

類比佈局之所以難以自動化，是因為其中大量知識從未被寫下來：對稱、匹配、鄰近性、寄生效應敏感度。我們將這些限制形式化並自動產生符合的佈局 —— 對稱島擺置、共質心結構、考慮寄生效應的電容尺寸設計 —— 這些成果同時產出頂尖論文與多項美國專利。

Physical design automation

實體設計自動化

Placement, legalization and routing at modern densities. Recent work covers multibit flip-flop banking for power and timing co-optimization, GPU-accelerated logic rewriting, and patch generation under resource constraints — several of which took top places in the IEEE/ACM ICCAD CAD Contest.

現代密度下的擺置、合法化與繞線。近期工作包括多位元正反器合併以共同最佳化功耗與時序、GPU 加速的邏輯改寫，以及資源受限下的修補生成 —— 其中多項在 IEEE/ACM ICCAD CAD 競賽中名列前茅。

3D integration and advanced packaging

三維積體電路與先進封裝

Stacking dies changes what counts as a good floorplan. We work on power delivery and routing across redistribution layers, including Voronoi-diagram-based generation of multiple power planes for 3D ICs.

晶片堆疊改變了「好的平面規劃」的定義。我們研究跨重佈線層的供電與繞線，包含以 Voronoi 圖為基礎的三維積體電路多重電源平面生成。

Schematic understanding and document analysis

電路圖理解與文件分析

Decades of circuit knowledge sit locked in images — scanned schematics, datasheet figures, patent drawings. Netlistify, recipient of the 2025 MLCAD Best Artifact Award, turns circuit schematics back into netlists with deep learning, making that archive machine-readable.

數十年的電路知識被鎖在圖片裡 —— 掃描的電路圖、規格書插圖、專利圖說。獲得 2025 MLCAD 最佳成果獎的 Netlistify 以深度學習將電路圖轉換回網表，讓這些資料重新能被機器讀取。
